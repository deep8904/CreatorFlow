# CreatorFlow — UI/UX Designer Brief

**This is a content and functionality brief, not a design direction.** Every layout, composition, spacing, and visual decision is yours to make creatively. Attached separately: the existing design token file (colors, type scale, spacing, radii, shadows) as available material — use it as you see fit; you're not required to use every token or follow any layout pattern described in it.

## The Product, in One Paragraph

CreatorFlow is a free tool that helps content creators run the business side of their work: tracking brand sponsorship deals from first email to paid invoice, capturing and developing content ideas, repurposing published videos into new content, and seeing how their channel is actually performing — all in one place, for free, with no cut taken from any deal. It connects to Gmail and YouTube. The audience is creators who are serious about their channel but not yet earning six figures — people currently juggling Gmail, spreadsheets, and memory to run their creator business.

## Tone

Direct, plain, respectful of the creator's time. Not corporate, not cutesy. This is a tool for someone doing real work, not a lifestyle brand. Avoid hype language ("supercharge," "10x," "unlock your potential").

## 1. Landing / Home

**Purpose:** Explain what this is and get a creator to sign up, in under a minute.

**Content to use:**
- Headline: "Run your creator business without losing your Gmail to it."
- Subheadline: "Track brand deals from first email to paid invoice, capture ideas before they slip away, and see what's actually working — free, with no cut taken from your deals, ever."
- Primary CTA: "Start free"
- Secondary CTA: "See how it works"
- Trust line: "Free means free. No percentage of your deals. No credit card to start."

**Feature highlights (three or four, your call on how many to show):**
- "Deals that don't get lost" — Connect Gmail and brand sponsorship emails get surfaced automatically, tracked from inquiry to paid.
- "Ideas, captured on the spot" — Write down a content idea the second you have it, before it's gone.
- "Know what's working" — See your YouTube performance next to your deal pipeline, not in a separate tab.
- "Never locked in" — Export everything you've put in, any time, no questions asked.

**Social proof / trust section:**
- "Built for creators who are serious about the business side, not just the top 1%."

## 2. How It Works

**Purpose:** Show the actual workflow, for someone who wants more than the landing page pitch before signing up.

**Content to use (a simple numbered flow, however you choose to present it):**
1. "Connect Gmail and YouTube" — Takes two minutes. Nothing is shared or sold. Disconnect any time.
2. "Brand deals get sorted for you" — Sponsorship emails are pulled out of your regular inbox noise into one pipeline view.
3. "Capture and develop your ideas" — Turn a passing thought into a scheduled piece of content.
4. "See the full picture" — Your deals, your ideas, and your channel performance, in one dashboard.

## 3. Pricing ("Free")

**Purpose:** Remove any doubt that this is genuinely free, and explain what "free" includes.

**Content to use:**
- Headline: "Free. Actually free."
- Body: "CreatorFlow doesn't take a percentage of your brand deals, and it doesn't charge a subscription for the core product. Connect your accounts, track your deals, capture your ideas — no cost, no catch."
- What's included list: unlimited deals, unlimited ideas and drafts, YouTube analytics, team access for you and one collaborator, monthly AI usage allowance (contract review, reply drafts, repurposing suggestions)
- Honest line about limits: "AI features (contract review, reply drafting, repurposing) have a generous monthly allowance on the free plan so the product stays sustainable for everyone using it."

## 4. Onboarding

**Purpose:** Get a new user from signup to a working Dashboard, fast, without forcing integrations.

**Step 1 — Welcome:**
- "Let's get your creator business set up. This takes about five minutes — and you can skip any step and come back later."

**Step 2 — Connect YouTube (skippable):**
- "Connect YouTube to see your performance alongside everything else."
- Skip link text: "Skip for now"

**Step 3 — Connect Gmail (skippable):**
- "Connect Gmail so brand deal emails get sorted automatically. We only look at sponsorship-related emails — we never read, delete, or send anything without your approval."
- Skip link text: "Skip for now"

**Step 4 — Done:**
- "You're set up. Here's your Dashboard."

## 5. Dashboard

**Purpose:** One glance tells the creator what needs their attention today.

**Content needed:**
- Section: "Needs your attention" — deals awaiting a reply, contracts awaiting review
- Section: "Recent ideas" — latest captured ideas
- Section: "This week's performance" — a brief YouTube snapshot
- Empty state (new user, nothing connected): "Nothing here yet. Connect Gmail to start tracking deals, or [add your first idea] to get going."

## 6. Deals

**Purpose:** The core workflow — every brand deal from first contact to paid.

**Content needed:**
- Pipeline stage labels: Inbound, Negotiating, Contracted, Delivered, Paid
- Empty state: "No deals yet. Connect Gmail to auto-detect sponsorship emails, or add a deal manually."
- "Add deal manually" button text
- Deal detail fields, in plain user-facing language (not technical field names): Brand name, Contact, What you're delivering, Rate, Due date, Status, Notes
- Contract review result labels: "Needs a closer look" (critical), "Worth checking" (risk), "For your information" (info) — plain language, not developer-speak
- AI reply draft disclaimer text: "This is a draft based on your rate card. Nothing sends until you approve it."

## 7. Ideas

**Purpose:** Fast capture, simple tracking.

**Content needed:**
- Status labels: New, In Progress, Scheduled, Done
- Empty state: "No ideas yet. The next one you have — write it down here before it disappears."
- "New idea" button text
- Fields: Title, Notes, Tags

## 8. Drafts

**Purpose:** Develop an idea into something ready to film or publish.

**Content needed:**
- Empty state: "No drafts yet. Turn one of your ideas into a draft to start writing."
- Link back to originating idea, labeled plainly: "From: [idea title]"

## 9. Repurpose

**Purpose:** Take a published video and get suggestions for turning it into more content.

**Content needed:**
- Prompt: "Paste a YouTube link, or pick one of your connected channel's videos."
- Result section labels: "Summary," "Clip-worthy moments," "Social post ideas," "Blog post outline"
- Honest disclaimer: "These are AI-generated starting points — review before you publish."

## 10. Automations

**Purpose:** Let simple, repetitive actions happen without manual work.

**Content needed:**
- Pre-built automation names and descriptions (plain language), e.g.:
  - "New sponsorship email → Create a deal" — "When Gmail detects a new brand deal email, automatically create a deal card for it."
  - "Deal marked Paid → Archive contract" — "Keep your Deals view focused on what's active."
- Empty/intro state: "Automations save you the repetitive steps. Turn on the ones that fit how you work."

## 11. Analytics

**Purpose:** Understand channel performance without digging through YouTube Studio.

**Content needed:**
- Section labels: Views, Watch time, Subscribers, Top videos
- Empty state (YouTube not connected): "Connect YouTube to see your performance here."
- Date range comparison labels: "This month," "Last month," "Custom range"

## 12. Team

**Purpose:** Bring in a manager or collaborator without giving up control.

**Content needed:**
- Empty state: "It's just you right now. Invite a collaborator to help manage deals and automations."
- Role labels and plain-language descriptions: "Owner — full access, including billing and team management." / "Member — can manage deals, ideas, and automations, but not settings or billing."
- Invite flow copy: "Invite by email" input, pending-invite state: "Invited — waiting for them to accept"

## 13. Settings

**Purpose:** Manage the account, integrations, and data.

**Content needed:**
- Sections: Profile, Connected accounts (Gmail, YouTube — each with Connect/Disconnect and a plain status line like "Connected as yourname@gmail.com"), Team, Data export, Delete account
- Data export copy: "Download everything you've put into CreatorFlow — deals, ideas, drafts, and settings — as files you can keep, any time."
- Delete account copy: "This permanently deletes your account and data. You can export your data first below."

## Notes for the Designer

- Every empty state above needs to actually appear designed, not be an afterthought — a large share of a new user's first session is empty states (nothing connected yet, no deals yet, no ideas yet).
- The Deals and Onboarding flows are the two screens most likely to determine whether someone becomes an active user — they deserve the most design attention.
- No further content will be dictated beyond what's above — where you need placeholder data (sample deals, sample analytics numbers) to design realistic screens, use your judgment to invent something plausible.
